Delete your EchoSteps account and data

EchoSteps: Speech & Imitation Lab

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open EchoSteps and tap the lock at the top right of the main screen.
  2. Answer the question for grown-ups.
  3. Go to Family & account and tap Delete account and all data.
  4. If you added an email and password, enter the password to confirm.

This permanently deletes every child profile and all practice history from the device and from the cloud backup, and deletes the sign-in. It cannot be undone.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app or the device, email us from the email address you used to save progress, at the address shown under Developer contact on EchoSteps' Google Play page, with the subject "Delete my EchoSteps account". We will delete the account and all its data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • The sign-in (anonymous ID, and the email address and password if one was added).
  • Every child profile: nickname, age group, buddy, settings.
  • All practice history: sessions, counts, practised and mastered sounds, unlocked cards.

Nothing is kept afterwards. EchoSteps never stores audio, so there are no recordings to delete.
